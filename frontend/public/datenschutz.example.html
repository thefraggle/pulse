Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how we collect and use your personal data.

1. Data Collection

When you use this app, we process the following data:

  • Server Logs: IP address, browser type, timestamp (usually collected by the hosting provider).
  • Session Data: Anything you submit to a room (poll votes, questions, words) is stored in our local database.

2. Cookies / Local Storage

We use Local Storage to store your authentication tokens (if you log in as a host) and to prevent multiple votes on polls from the same device.

3. Your Rights

You have the right to request information, deletion, or correction of your data at any time. Contact us at your@email.com.
